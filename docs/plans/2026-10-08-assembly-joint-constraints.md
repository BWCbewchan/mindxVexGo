# Assembly joint constraints

Preserve existing analytic joints when editing with drag, transform handles or coordinates. Correct lateral displacement to the nearest compatible hole on the same target, retain coaxial insertion adjustment, and reject edits that break another edge. Snap nearby loose parts on release; staging away from a joint remains possible. Explicit Unlink is required to detach an established joint.

Use current CAD references without inventing square-bore or electronics socket compatibility. Unsupported references remain unresolved. Apply joint correction before mesh collision protection, with rollback and Undo. Test sideways correction, adjacent-hole selection, insertion, target movement and incompatible references. No automatic physical-build certification.
